# Coastal Geography & Ports of the Arabian Sea

## Regional Coastal Sectors
1. **Gujarat & Saurashtra Coast (20.0°N – 24.5°N)**:
   - Features wide continental shelves, high tidal ranges, and rich mudflat estuaries.
   - Major Ports: Kandla (Deendayal Port), Mundra, Pipavav, Hazira.
   - Key Fishing Harbours: Porbandar, Veraval, Jakhau, Mangrol, Okha.
   - Known for demersal fish, ribbonfish, croakers, pomfrets, and Bombay duck.

2. **Maharashtra & Konkan Coast (15.8°N – 20.0°N)**:
   - Moderate shelf width with deep submarine canyons off Mumbai.
   - Major Ports: Mumbai Port, JNPT (Jawaharlal Nehru Port).
   - Key Coastal Harbours: Alibaug, Dighi, Dabhol, Jaigad, Ratnagiri, Vijaydurg, Devgad, Malvan.
   - Highly productive pelagic waters for Indian mackerel, seerfish, and squid, with fishing grounds typically located 30 to 90 km offshore beyond coastal shipping corridors.

3. **Goa & Karnataka Coast (12.7°N – 15.8°N)**:
   - High seasonal rainfall and strong river discharge into the sea.
   - Major Ports: Mormugao Port (Goa), New Mangalore Port (Karnataka).
   - Key Fishing Harbours: Panaji, Karwar, Tadri, Honnavar, Bhatkal, Kundapura, Malpe.
   - Malpe and Karwar host extensive purse-seine fleets targeting sardines and mackerel.

4. **Kerala & Malabar Coast (8.0°N – 12.7°N)**:
   - Narrow continental shelf with intense seasonal wind-driven summer upwelling.
   - Major Ports: Kochi (Cochin) Port.
   - Key Fishing Harbours: Beypore (Kozhikode), Munambam, Kollam, Neendakara, Vizhinjam (Thiruvananthapuram).
   - World-renowned sardine and tuna fishing grounds, historically supported by strong coastal mudbanks (Chakara) and upwelling fronts.

## Maritime Zones
- **Territorial Waters**: Coastal baseline up to 12 nautical miles (~22 km).
- **Contiguous Zone**: 12 to 24 nautical miles (~44 km).
- **Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)**: Out to 200 nautical miles (~370 km), over which coastal states exercise sovereign rights over living marine resources.

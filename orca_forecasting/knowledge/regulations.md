# Maritime Boundaries and Fisheries Regulations in the Arabian Sea

## 1. The 12 Nautical Mile (~22.2 km) Territorial Waters Limit
Under the Maritime Zones of India Act (1976) and state-level Marine Fisheries Regulation Acts (MFRA):
- **0 to 12 Nautical Miles (Territorial Waters)**: Represents sovereign territorial sea. Under coastal fisheries rules across Gujarat, Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, and Kerala, the inshore coastal zone (typically up to 12 NM) is strictly reserved for traditional, artisanal, and small non-motorized/motorized canoes and boats to protect traditional fishermen's livelihoods and nearshore nursery habitats.
- **Beyond 12 Nautical Miles (Contiguous Zone & EEZ)**: Deep-sea mechanized vessels, pelagic trawlers, and commercial longliners are authorized to fish in these deeper pelagic waters extending across the Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) up to 200 NM (~370 km).
- **ORCA's 12 NM Hard Filter**: ORCA enforces a strict computational rule that filters candidate fishing spots for commercial fishing vessels so that all recommended Potential Fishing Zones (PFZs) lie seaward of the 12 NM (~22.2 km) line, preventing illegal encroachment into artisanal coastal zones.

## 2. Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ)
- India's Arabian Sea EEZ encompasses over 860,000 square kilometers, providing rich fishing grounds for tuna, mackerel, sardines, cephalopods, and carangids.
- Foreign vessels are prohibited from commercial fishing within India's 200 NM EEZ without formal bilateral licensing from the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying.

## 3. Seasonal Monsoon Fishing Ban (Uniform Ban)
- **West Coast (Arabian Sea)**: An annual uniform fishing ban of 61 days (typically from June 1 to July 31) is strictly enforced for mechanized vessels to allow fish stocks to spawn and regenerate during the peak southwest monsoon upwelling period.
- Traditional artisanal non-motorized craft are exempt from the monsoon ban, allowing localized subsistence fishing.

## 4. Marine Protected Areas & Shipping Corridors
- Commercial trawling is prohibited inside designated marine parks (such as the Malvan Marine Sanctuary in Maharashtra and Gulf of Kutch Marine National Park in Gujarat).
- Designated Traffic Separation Schemes (TSS) around major port approaches (Mumbai, JNPT, Kandla, Kochi) restrict static gillnetting to prevent collisions with container and tanker traffic.

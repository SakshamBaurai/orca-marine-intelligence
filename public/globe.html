🌐 What are ORCA Grid Points?

Each grid point is a discrete Copernicus Marine (CMEMS) numerical model sampling cell (~35 km / 0.333° resolution). They represent continuous observation nodes across the Indian EEZ—not all grid nodes are fishing spots.

🎯 3-Tier Potential Fishing Zone (PFZ) System

HIGH / FAVORABLE Score ≥ 68, Health ≥ 42. Prominent cyan beacon. Optimal thermal window (26.5–29.2°C), active upwelling front, and high chlorophyll-a forage (>0.5 mg/m³).
MODERATE / POTENTIAL Score ≥ 50, Health ≥ 32. Amber beacon. Favorable primary forage or thermal conditions with stable secondary parameters.
LESS FAVORABLE Score ≥ 38. Subtle slate marker with amber border. Viable for opportunistic operations; clicking displays specific limiting factors (e.g. low chlorophyll or thermal deviation).

📊 Core Parameters & Signs

  • Water Temperature (°C): Sea surface temperature measured by Copernicus satellites. Best fishing is in the 26°C–29.5°C sweet spot.
  • Water Temp Change (°C): Temperature variation (cooler water often indicates upwelling rich with fish food).
  • Fish Food / Plankton (mg/m³): Chlorophyll-a micro-plant density that attracts fish schools like sardines, mackerel, and tuna.
  • Sea Level & Swell (cm): Altimeter sea surface height; helps detect offshore currents and productive feeding edges.
  • Ocean Health Score (0–100): Overall AI ecosystem health score; higher scores mean clean, productive, lively waters.
  • 12 NM Small Boat Zone (~22 km): Coastal belt reserved exclusively for traditional artisanal fishers. Commercial zones are beyond.
